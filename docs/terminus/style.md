# Terminus Series Style

Beyond the site's house style (plain English, concise, no hype), every post
follows two rules:

1. **Assume no prior knowledge.** Explain each concept in plain language
   before using its name — tidal locking, terminator, resonance, Doppler,
   handover, all of them. A curious reader with no physics or networking
   background should never hit a wall. Later posts may link back to the post
   that introduced a concept instead of re-explaining it in full.
2. **Keep the fiction alive.** Each post carries the in-universe frame
   (Terminus Systems answering the AI's RFP) and at least a scene's worth of
   science-fiction texture — a view from the surface, the AI's voice, life
   along the twilight band. The fiction serves the engineering: use it to
   make a number felt, not to decorate.

Posts are staged on the site repo's `terminus` branch, which is itself the
draft space — individual posts do not carry `draft = true`.
